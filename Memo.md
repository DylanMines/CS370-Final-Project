# The User
Our user is any person who lives in a home, big or small. The place: any entrance of a home. There are many who hear a strange sound in the night, and it gives them a fright. In these instances, one might want to know if their roommate was microwaving hot pockets at 3 am and didn't turn the microwave off, or if they should call the police for an intruder. Such a user may look into an internet connected camera, which becomes an investigation quickly. Take another user who is a control freak, and always wants to know when someone enters or leaves their home. While this user could use cameras and AI detection, a much simpler solution is possible. They would place a device in their entrance-way, and forget. No internet setups, no AI or cloud companies, just plain data collection and a way to show it. 
# The Problem
In the US, a home break-in occurs, on average, every 26 seconds, according the FBI. A break in without a security system leaves the homeowner, at home or away, more vulnerable. Where an alarm system steps in is to give an invisible advantage to the owner, providing a defense, and time. A robust system that will always log, no matter what, provides a sense of security in that you'll never miss anything.
# Why Such a Device?
This is a device which always lives at the entrance, never sleeps, and cannot fail. The sensors detect the physical presense of someone in their wake, and in such a way that provides useful information. Your phone *might* be able to use its camera to find such data, but then again how often do you strap your phone to a wall by your front door? The logging stays on-device, securely, and won't be erased. No cloud companies using camera data, no required internet connection.
